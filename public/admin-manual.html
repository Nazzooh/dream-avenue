03 / Your trusted network

Make introductions that create value.

Partner Network is the shared directory for people who help turn an event idea into a complete experience.

Partner Network

Find & connect

Search a business or person by service, category, contact or location. Keep preferred suppliers current, store multiple contacts, and use phone/email links for quick follow-up.

  • Use clear categories such as Decor & Florals, Transport, Artists or Catering.
  • Mark only genuinely trusted, active suppliers as Preferred.
  • Keep internal notes useful and client-safe.
/admin/partners

Referrals

Track value

Log the introduction every time Dream Avenue connects a client or prospect to a partner. Move it through Introduced, In discussion, Converted or Not converted.

  • Record the requested service and a recognisable client reference.
  • Managers and Coordinators can progress a referral.
  • Owner and Developer see financial totals and may export the report.

04 / Work with a memory

Tasks for action. Activity for clarity.

Keep execution visible to the right person, while maintaining a trustworthy record of major changes.

Team Tasks

Everyone

Make tasks specific: “Confirm floral setup time” gives someone a clear outcome; “Florals” does not. Set priority, due date and assignee. Use In progress while it is actively underway.

/admin/tasks

Activity & Audit

Owner + Developer

Use Activity to understand what changed, when and by whom. Filter, inspect the detail drawer, and export CSV when a formal history is needed. It is an immutable record, not a note-taking tool.

/admin/activity

How do we add a new staff member?

They first create an account using the same email they submit in their request. Then Owner or Developer opens /admin/requests, verifies their identity and need, and approves the request. Approval deliberately grants Coordinator—the least-privileged starting role.

Why does a page say Permission required?

That message is expected when a role does not have that capability. Do not share accounts or ask someone else to perform an action without context. Ask Owner or Developer to review whether the role itself should change.

What needs extra care?

Permanent deletion, converted referrals and public content all deserve a second check. Confirm you are on the right record, understand whether the action is reversible, and avoid removing real client history while testing.

What if a page is empty or does not work?

An empty state may be correct: it can mean there are no matching records. If the page is unavailable, reload once and sign in again. If it persists, share the URL, your role, approximate time, action attempted, error text and a screenshot with the Developer.

How should I report a design issue?

Include the route, device width, a screenshot and the exact action. Good reports are specific: “At 375px, the booking filter hides behind the mobile navigation after opening the drawer.”
